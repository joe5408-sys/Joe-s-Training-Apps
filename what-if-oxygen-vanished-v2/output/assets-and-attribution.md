# Figures, sources, assets and licences — "What If All the Oxygen Vanished?" v2 (live waterfront)

**Thought experiment.** Matter cannot simply disappear. Each caption carries a small tag:
**REAL DATA** (published value) · **ROUGH ESTIMATE · OUR CALCULATION** · **THOUGHT EXPERIMENT**.
Page-level citations were not re-checked during this build.

## Format note

The *format* (one real place at human scale, a live readout, short present-tense captions, calm-then-escalating
pacing, and an honest closing footnote) was inspired by a reference short the user supplied. No footage, art,
audio, text or branding from that video is used. The waterfront, buildings, footbridge, boats and people are an
original, stylised low-poly illustration built in `src/world3d.py`, loosely inspired by the Kuching waterfront on
the Sarawak River; it is **not** an accurate model of any real building or bridge.

## Timing on screen

- The **30-second countdown** is real time on screen (00:30 → 00:00).
- After T = 0 the readout shows **physical time in slow motion**: about 10 seconds of events stretched over
  ~40 seconds of video, labelled SLOW MOTION. The exact speed of each effect is illustrative, not simulated.
