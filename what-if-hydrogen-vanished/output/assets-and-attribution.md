# Figures, sources, assets and licences — "What If Hydrogen Vanished?"

**This is a thought experiment.** Matter cannot simply disappear (mass–energy is conserved), so there is no
real-world model of the event. The video separates three kinds of statement, each with its own on-screen badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REAL DATA** | A published, measured or standard value |
| **ROUGH ESTIMATE · OUR CALCULATION** | Simple arithmetic on published values (working shown below) |
| **THOUGHT EXPERIMENT** | The impossible premise and its direct consequences |

Page-level citations were not re-checked during this build; the references are where to verify each figure.

## 2. Simplifications to be aware of

- "Instantly, everywhere" ignores relativity of simultaneity; it is part of the impossible premise.
- The ocean-pressure figure is an **upper-bound style estimate**, not a forecast. Real outcomes (explosive
  expansion, heating, oxygen reacting with rock, some gas escaping to space) are not modelled.
- What the Sun's remains would do (and look like) is not predicted. The video only uses its **remaining mass**,
  which is enough to show Earth becomes unbound.
- Percentages for the human body and Jupiter vary slightly between sources; the video uses rounded central values.
