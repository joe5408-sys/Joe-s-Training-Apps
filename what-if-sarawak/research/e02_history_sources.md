## Deliberately avoided
- 22 July 1963 ("Sarawak Day"): its name ("Independence" vs "Self-government") is disputed
  ([Borneo Post](https://www.theborneopost.com/2020/07/22/wan-junaidi-sarawak-gained-self-governance-not-independence/)), so the film does not use it as the divergence point or mention it.
- MA63 rights disputes, oil and gas revenue, and real politicians — out of scope for a comedy and politically loaded.
- Any statement that an independent Sarawak would be richer or poorer: S7 shows both sides and ends with "No easy answer."
