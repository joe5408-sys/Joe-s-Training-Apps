# Alternate timeline (FICTION) — assumptions for E01

Everything after the divergence point is invented for comedy. Nothing here is a prediction or an
argument that this outcome was likely or better.

## Divergence point
- **Real:** May 1946, Council Negri passes the cession bill 19–16 (third reading); Sarawak becomes a
  British Crown Colony on 1 July 1946 (see `history_sources.md`, rows 3–4).
- **Fiction:** the same vote fails 16–19. Chosen because the real margin was narrow (3 votes), so the
  "what if" is easy to grasp. This is a storytelling choice, not a counterfactual analysis — we do not
  model *why* votes would change, or what Britain, Brunei, Malaya or Indonesia would have done next.

## What the film does **not** claim
- That Sarawak would be richer, poorer, happier or less happy (S7 asks the question and leaves it open).
- Anything about real current politicians or the Brooke Heritage Trust.
