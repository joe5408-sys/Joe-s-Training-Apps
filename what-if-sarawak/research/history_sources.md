# Historical sources — What If...? E01

**How these were checked (read this first).** The sandbox that produced this film could not open
sarawak.gov.my, museum.sarawak.gov.my, brooketrust.org or arkib.gov.my (network policy blocked them).
Claims below were checked with web search across **at least two independent sources each**, one of them
a primary or reference source where possible (UK Hansard, UN Treaty Series, Britannica). I did **not**
read the full text of every page. Before broadcast, confirm each row against a primary source
(Sarawak State Library / Sarawak Museum / National Archives of Malaysia).

## Visual representations — not factual claims
- **Map of Borneo / Sarawak** is hand-simplified from approximate coordinates, labelled on screen
  "map simplified, not to scale". The west→east highlight sweep only suggests that territory grew over
  decades; it does **not** show real boundary dates.
- **Portraits** of the three Rajahs are generic silhouettes, labelled "illustrative silhouettes". They are
  not likenesses.
- **The 19th-century schooner** is generic; no ship name is claimed.
- **Council Negri chamber** interior and member silhouettes are invented set design.

## Excluded on purpose
- No real living Brooke descendant is depicted, named or voiced.
- No statements about modern Sarawak economic figures (all economy content in S6 is labelled "imagined").
