## Sensitivity choices
- The ban comes from an invented "Noodle Board" and covers **all** kolo mee (pork and halal chicken), so there is no religious or ethnic angle.
- No real ministry, agency, officer or politician is shown. The officer's uniform and title are invented.
- Rajah Edmund (E01) appears as a fictional cameo, labelled.
- Narration line "Ban something people love… and they'll find another way" is a comedic observation, not a policy claim.
