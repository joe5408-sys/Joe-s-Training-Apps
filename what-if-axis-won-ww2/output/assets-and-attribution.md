# Assets, audio, voice and licensing — "What If Japan and Germany Won World War II?"

Every image, map graphic, music cue and sound effect in this video was **generated by code in this
project**. No stock footage, archival photographs, film clips, sampled music or third-party sound
effects were used. The only third-party inputs are listed in section 1.

## 2. Voice (narration)

- **Engine:** Kokoro-82M (open-weight, Apache-2.0), run locally with `kokoro-onnx` (MIT). No paid API, no account.
- **Voice:** `bm_george` — a stock synthetic British-English male voice that ships with the model.
  It is **not a clone or imitation of any real, identifiable person**, and no voice cloning was done.
- **Pronunciation:** espeak-ng phonemisation (GPL-3.0 tool, used only at build time; the generated audio is not GPL-encumbered).
  "Sarawak" was manually corrected to the local stress *sa-RAH-wak*; "Malaysia" was fixed to remove a linking "r".
  Years are spoken as "nineteen forty-five", etc.
- **Disclosure:** the narration is AI-generated. Some platforms ask creators to label synthetic or altered
  media; adding "Narration: AI voice" to the post description is recommended.

## 5. Historical basis for the "ACTUAL HISTORY" statements

These are standard, well-established facts. The references below are where to verify them; page-level
citations were **not** re-checked during this build.

| On-screen / spoken claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| Germany surrendered in May 1945; Japan announced surrender in August 1945 (formal signing 2 Sept 1945) | Standard chronology, e.g. Imperial War Museums; U.S. National Archives (Japanese Instrument of Surrender) |
| 1939 war begins in Europe; Dec 1941 war spreads to the Pacific | Standard chronology. Note: the Sino-Japanese war had already begun in 1937 |
| Nazi regime murdered six million Jews and millions of others | United States Holocaust Memorial Museum, *Holocaust Encyclopedia*, "Documenting Numbers of Victims of the Holocaust and Nazi Persecution" |
| Japanese rule in occupied Asia was harsh but varied by place; resources (rice, rubber, oil, tin) extracted; hundreds of thousands forced into labour | P. H. Kratoska (ed.), *Asian Labor in the Wartime Japanese Empire* (2005); P. H. Kratoska, *The Japanese Occupation of Malaya 1941–1945* (1998) |
| Burma–Thailand Railway built 1942–43 with forced labour (Asian labourers and Allied POWs) | Commonwealth War Graves Commission; Australian War Memorial. Death-toll estimates vary, so **no figure is shown** |
| Germany and Japan were distant allies with different ambitions | e.g. G. L. Weinberg, *A World at Arms* (1994) on limited Axis coordination. This is a mainstream interpretation, not a single fact |
| Japan occupied Sarawak, Dec 1941 – Sept 1945 (landing at Miri 16 Dec 1941; Kuching taken 24 Dec 1941; surrender at Kuching Sept 1945) | Ooi Keat Gin, *The Japanese Occupation of Borneo, 1941–45* (2011); Australian War Memorial |
| Shortages, strict control and fear under the occupation | Ooi Keat Gin (2011) |
| Malaysia formed 16 September 1963 (Malaya, Singapore, Sabah, Sarawak) | Standard chronology. The highlight includes Singapore, which left in 1965 |

## 6. Restrictions, uncertainties and honest caveats

- **Speculation is speculation.** The settlement, the zones, the three-power rivalry and every post-1945
  outcome are imagined. They are labelled ALTERNATE HISTORY / FICTIONAL SCENARIO / SPECULATIVE MAP on screen.
- **Speculative zones are deliberately blurred blobs**, not borders. They are not historical and not a prediction.
- **Modern borders on "actual history" maps.** The resource map and the Sarawak zoom use present-day Natural
  Earth borders (e.g. Malaysia and Indonesia did not exist as states in 1942). They are there only for orientation.
- **Illustrations are "historically inspired".** The Kuching waterfront, the fort and the shophouses are simplified
  silhouettes, not accurate reconstructions. The scene is labelled HISTORICALLY INSPIRED ILLUSTRATION.
- **No symbols of the Nazi or Imperial Japanese regimes** are shown. Flags in the meeting scene are plain, patterned
  banners in the zone colours.
- The **music and sound effects are synthetic**. They are free of third-party rights, but they are simpler than a
  library orchestral score.
