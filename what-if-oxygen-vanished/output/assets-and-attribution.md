# Figures, sources, assets and licences — "What If Oxygen Vanished?"

**Thought experiment.** Matter cannot simply disappear (mass–energy is conserved). Deadpan jokes sit on the
figures below; each card carries a badge: **REAL DATA** (published value) · **ROUGH ESTIMATE · OUR CALCULATION**
(arithmetic on published values) · **THOUGHT EXPERIMENT** (the premise and its consequences).
Page-level citations were not re-checked during this build.

## 2. Simplifications

- How far the ground would fall, how the planet would re-settle, and how surface gravity would change are **not
  modelled**; the video only states the mass lost.
- The hydrogen "sky" and body-pressure figures are order-of-magnitude estimates, labelled as such.
- "Instantly, everywhere" is part of the impossible premise.
