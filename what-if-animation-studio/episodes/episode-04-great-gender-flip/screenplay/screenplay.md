## Design notes
- Every reversal is shown symmetrically (Alex gets one rule set, Maya gets the mirror set), so the joke lands on the rules, not on either sex.
- Logic check: post-flip, Alex (now physically female) receives the expectations formerly aimed at men, and Maya (now physically male) receives those formerly aimed at women. This holds in S03, S05, S07.
- A visual surprise lands every ~3–7 s (see beats in `timeline.json`).
