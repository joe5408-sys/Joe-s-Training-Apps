# Figures, sources, assets and licences — "What If Hydrogen Vanished?" v2 (countdown + dark humour)

**Thought experiment.** Matter cannot simply disappear (mass–energy is conserved). The jokes are deadpan and
aimed at the situation or the viewer; each one sits on a figure below. Badges on screen:
**REAL DATA** (published value) · **ROUGH ESTIMATE · OUR CALCULATION** (arithmetic on published values) ·
**THOUGHT EXPERIMENT** (the premise and its consequences). Page-level citations were not re-checked.

## 1. Figures carried over from v1

Sources and working are in `../what-if-hydrogen-vanished/output/assets-and-attribution.md`:
74% of ordinary matter (by mass), 92% of atoms, ~62% of the body's atoms (~10% of its mass), ~60% of the body is
water, 1.34 billion km³ / 1.4 × 10²¹ kg of ocean water, 1.24 × 10²¹ kg of oxygen left (≈240× the atmosphere's mass,
~240 atm), Venus 92 bar, 600 million t of hydrogen fused per second, 8 min 19 s, 29.8 / 42.1 / 22–23 km/s,
Jupiter 318 Earth masses (~¾ hydrogen), dark energy 68.5% + dark matter 26.5%, hydrogen ≈3.6% of the universe.

## 3. Design notes

- **Countdown stamps** (T + 0.000 s, T + 8 MIN 19 S, T + 1 YEAR) mark when each effect starts. "T + SECONDS?"
  on the fire scene has a question mark on purpose: its timing is not known.
- **Non-graphic**: the body is a cloud of coloured atom dots throughout; the "pop" is a dust-and-gas burst.
- The phone screen ("Are you still watching?") is a generic, fictional UI, not any real app.
